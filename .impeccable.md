## Design Context

### Users

Livefeed is for streamers and developer-creators who work from a terminal and want to monitor
YouTube or Twitch chat without keeping another browser window open. The primary job is to sign in
once, start the CLI, and trust it to find the current stream and stay connected.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, and utilitarian. The product should feel dependable and technically mature, with the
confidence of a well-made command-line tool.

### Aesthetic Direction

Tactical terminal documentation: one continuous charcoal substrate, compressed industrial
typography, strict technical grids, one functional status green, and realistic terminal output.
Avoid startup-style heroes, feature cards, decorative gradients, glow effects, excessive rounding,
fake depth, and copy that sounds generated.

### Design Principles

1. Show the product instead of decorating around it.
2. Make every word and visual mark carry information.
3. Use hierarchy, spacing, and rules before containers or effects.
4. Keep motion quiet, brief, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
5. Adapt composition at each breakpoint instead of merely shrinking it.
